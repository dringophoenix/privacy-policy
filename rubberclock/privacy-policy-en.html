Privacy Policy

Updated Date: June 23, 2026

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

App Name: Rubber Clock

Rubber Clock (“this App”, “the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) values your personal information and privacy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information, as well as the rights you have in relation to your information.

Please read this Policy carefully before using this App. When you first use the App, use related features, or before we request system permissions, we will explain the relevant information processing rules through in-app prompts, system permission pop-ups, or other reasonable methods. If you do not agree to the relevant processing, you may be unable to use features that depend on such information, but this will not affect your use of other basic features that do not depend on such information.

1. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our processing of personal information, please contact us through the following method:

Email: support@rubberclock.com

2. Information We Collect

To provide the features of this App, we may collect or use the following information:

1. Account and Identifier Information

  • Anonymous account identifiers, or user identifiers provided by Apple when you choose to use Sign in with Apple;
  • Device identifiers and login session information, used for security verification, abuse prevention, and quota management.

2. Content You Actively Provide

  • Text descriptions and options you enter when customizing a clock face;
  • Reference images you select from your photo library and upload;
  • Clock face styles, pet materials, and related configurations you create, some of which may be stored locally on your device.

Please avoid uploading sensitive content such as identity documents, bank cards, medical records, or clear facial images of minors. If you actively upload images containing personal information or sensitive personal information, we will process them only for the purpose of completing your requested AI customization, image processing, or related features.

3. Business and Transaction Records

  • Records related to AI generation tasks and results;
  • Rubber balance, consumption, and top-up records;
  • Apple transaction receipt information required for in-app purchases.

4. Device and Log Information

  • App version, operation time, service operation logs, and similar information, used to ensure service stability, security, and troubleshooting.

We do not actively collect sensitive personal information unrelated to the functions of this App, such as precise location, contacts, or calendar information. We also do not display targeted advertising in the App or use cross-app tracking.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only for the following purposes:

  • Providing core features: clock display, AI customization, material generation, clock face saving and restoration;
  • Account and security: login, session maintenance, anti-spam and abuse control;
  • Billing and quota management: free trial usage, Rubber balance, consumption records, in-app purchase receipt verification and crediting;
  • Service improvement and troubleshooting: analyzing operation logs within the necessary scope to ensure service stability;
  • Compliance with legal obligations: providing necessary information when required by laws and regulations.

4. AI Customization and Entrusted Processing

To provide AI customization and related features, when you actively use such features, we will send necessary information to our servers and entrust cooperating cloud service providers and artificial intelligence service providers to process such information.

The purposes of processing include:

  • Account authentication and business data storage;
  • Text optimization, image generation, video generation, and image processing;
  • Security, billing, and troubleshooting directly related to the above services.

The information involved may include the text descriptions and generation parameters you enter, reference images you actively upload, generation task results, and account identifiers, device identifiers, or session information associated with the request.

The above information is used only to provide the features of this App to you. It will not be sold to third parties and will not be used for targeted advertising unrelated to this App.

If you stop using the AI customization feature, we will no longer send the above information for new AI generation requests. Service logs, transaction records, or security records that have been retained in accordance with the law may not be deleted immediately within the statutory retention period.

5. Third-Party Services

To provide certain features of this App, we may integrate the following third-party services:

1. Apple

Purpose: Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, transaction verification, and system permission management.

Information involved may include: user identifiers provided by Apple, transaction receipts, order status, and similar information.

2. Cloud Service Providers

Purpose: account authentication, business data storage, service operation, security risk control, and logging.

Information involved may include: anonymous user identifiers, login sessions, Rubber balance, generation records, order records, service logs, and similar information.

3. AI Service Providers

Purpose: text optimization, image generation, video generation, and image processing.

Information involved may include: text descriptions you enter, generation parameters, reference images you actively upload, generation task results, and anonymous account identifiers associated with the request.

We will require the above service providers to process relevant information only for the purpose of providing the features of this App and to take reasonable information security protection measures.

6. Information Sharing

Except in the following circumstances, we will not sell, rent, or illegally share your personal information with third parties:

  • Cloud service providers and AI service providers entrusted to provide the features of this App;
  • Services provided by Apple, such as login, in-app purchases, and transaction verification;
  • Where required by laws and regulations, or lawfully requested by judicial authorities or administrative authorities;
  • Where necessary to protect the legitimate rights and interests of you, us, or other users.

7. Information Storage and Retention

  • Your clock face configurations, drafts, and similar information may be stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App may result in loss of local data.
  • Service-related business data, such as accounts, quotas, orders, and generation records, is stored within the territory of the People’s Republic of China.
  • We retain information only for the period necessary to achieve the purposes described in this Policy. After that period, we will delete or anonymize the information, unless otherwise required by laws and regulations.
  • Transaction, accounting, security, log, and similar information may be retained for a necessary period according to laws and regulations, dispute resolution needs, or security audit requirements.

8. Your Rights

To the extent permitted by laws and regulations, you may:

  • Access and correct information related to your account;
  • Request deletion of server-side data related to your account;
  • Stop using the AI customization feature to stop new AI data processing;
  • Manage permissions such as photo library access through Apple system settings;
  • Cancel your account or request deletion of server-side data.

You may initiate account cancellation or data deletion requests through the settings entry in the App. If the App does not yet provide the corresponding entry, you may also send an email to support@rubberclock.com and explain your request along with verifiable account information, such as the user identifier suffix displayed in the App. We will respond and process your request within a reasonable period.

Please note that after account deletion or deletion of server-side data, some features may no longer be available. Completed Apple transaction records, legally retained records, accounting records, or security logs may not be deleted immediately.

9. Protection of Minors

This App is mainly intended for adults. If you are a minor, please read this Policy and use this App under the guidance of your guardian.

If a guardian believes that a minor has provided personal information to us without consent, the guardian may contact us at support@rubberclock.com. We will handle the matter in accordance with the law after verification.

10. Information Security

We take reasonable technical and management measures to protect information security, including but not limited to access control, transmission encryption, log auditing, and permission management.

However, internet transmission and storage cannot be guaranteed to be absolutely secure. Please properly protect your device, account, and related information, and avoid uploading sensitive content unrelated to the features of this App.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Policy from time to time and notify you through in-app display, page prompts, or other reasonable methods. If an update involves major changes to the purpose, method, or type of personal information processing, or to your rights, we will notify you in a more prominent manner.

If you continue to use this App, it means that you have read and understood the updated Policy.

12. Scope of Application

This Policy applies to this App client and related cloud services. Matters not covered herein shall be subject to the Rubber Clock User Service Agreement and applicable laws and regulations.
